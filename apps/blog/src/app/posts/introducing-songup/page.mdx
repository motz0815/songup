# Introducing SongUp

Every great party has a soundtrack — and until now, that soundtrack lived on one
person's phone. SongUp changes that. It turns any speaker into a shared jukebox
where your guests request and upvote songs straight from their phones, no app
install required.

## How it works

1. The host opens a room and connects it to their music.
2. Guests scan a QR code to join — instantly, right in the browser.
3. Everyone requests songs and upvotes their favorites.
4. The queue reorders itself so the crowd's favorites rise to the top.

## Why we built it

We kept running into the same problem at parties, bars, and events: the music
either got hijacked by one loud voice, or nobody wanted the responsibility of
being the DJ. SongUp makes the queue collaborative and fair, so the room decides
together what plays next.
