# How to Set Up a Song Request QR Code for Your Party or Venue

![Close-up of a person holding up a smartphone to scan a QR code.](https://images.pexels.com/photos/12935064/pexels-photo-12935064.jpeg)

QR code song requests have quietly become the standard way parties, bars, and
events handle music. No app download, no account, guests just scan and go.
Here's exactly how to set one up.

## Why QR codes work so well for this

A QR code removes every point of friction that kills guest participation:

- No app to download.
- No account to create.
- No need to know anyone's phone number or username.
- Works on any smartphone camera, instantly.

Compare that to alternatives like "add yourself to this Spotify playlist" (
requires an account and the right app) or "text me your song requests"
(requires the host to manually search and queue every single one). A QR code
that opens straight into a request page removes all of that.

## Step-by-step: setting up your own

1. **Open a room.** With [SongUp](https://songup.tv/host), click "Start a room" — no signup
   required to create one.
2. **Connect it to your sound system.** Play the room on the device connected
   to your speakers, TV, or PA system.
3. **Get your QR code.** SongUp generates one automatically for your room —
   scanning it takes guests straight into the request page in their browser.
4. **Display it prominently.** Print it large and place it:
   - On tables near the dance floor or bar
   - Near the entrance so it's the first thing guests see
   - On a TV or projector screen showing the live queue
5. **Announce it once.** A single "scan this to add your songs" from the host
   or MC is usually enough — word spreads fast once a few people try it.
6. **Set your limits.** Configure how many songs each guest can queue at once
   so no one person floods it.
7. **Add a fallback playlist.** So the music never stops even during a lull
   in requests.

## Tips for higher guest participation

- **Make the QR code big.** A tiny code on a business card gets missed —
  print it poster-size if you can.
- **Show the live queue on a screen.** Seeing your song land in the queue is
  genuinely satisfying and encourages more requests.
- **Place codes at natural waiting points** — the bar line, the bathroom line,
  the entrance — anywhere people have a spare 15 seconds.

## What happens after guests scan

With SongUp specifically, scanning the code takes guests straight into a
browser-based room (no app install) where they can search YouTube for any
song and add it to the queue — a few songs at a time each, so it stays fair.
Requests play in the order they're added, and on a Pro room the host can
still bump a song to play next, skip it, or remove it if needed.
