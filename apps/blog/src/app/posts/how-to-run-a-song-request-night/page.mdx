# How to run a song request night

A song request night is one of the easiest ways to turn a quiet weeknight into a
packed, music-driven event. Here's the playbook we've seen work best.

## Before the night

- **Pick a theme (optional).** Throwbacks, a specific decade, or "guilty
  pleasures" give guests an easy prompt.
- **Set up your room early.** Open a SongUp room and test the connection to your
  sound system before doors open.
- **Print the QR code big.** Put it on tables, the bar, and near the entrance so
  it's impossible to miss.

## During the night

- **Announce it once.** A single "scan to add your songs" from the host is
  usually all it takes.
- **Let the crowd curate.** Upvotes keep the energy honest — the songs the room
  actually wants rise to the top.
- **Keep a light touch.** As host you can always reorder or skip, but the less
  you intervene, the more ownership the crowd feels.

## After the night

Export the queue as inspiration for your house playlist, and note which themes
drew the biggest crowds so you can do it again.
